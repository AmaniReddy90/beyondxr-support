Help & troubleshooting

Here to help.

Start with the guidance below. For a problem that needs a closer look, report it through our support tracker.

Customize Home

Tap Arrange tools on Home. Hold and drag a tool onto another to reorder it. Use the tool menu to choose Compact, Wide or Tall. Customize Home controls section order and tool visibility.

Export a scan

Open Experimental Scanner, choose Library and open a saved scan. In 3D scene, scroll below the preview, choose a format and tap Export. After preparation, use the share sheet or Save to Files. USDZ preserves captured photo colors for compatible 3D and AR viewers. OBJ, PLY and STL export geometry; SVC preserves scene photographs and textures. A compact SVC import does not restore persistent positioning.

Crop a scene

Open Crop / trim scene from the 3D review. Adjust the boundaries to retain the desired area, then save. Saved trims apply to scene viewing and exports; the original scan remains unchanged. Cancel discards the current preview changes.

AR and scanning

Allow camera access when using camera features. Capture a well-lit space and move slowly with overlapping views. LiDAR capture requires supported hardware. Mirrors, thin edges and unseen surfaces can produce incomplete reconstruction.

Report a problem

Include the app version from Settings, iPhone model, iOS version, tool name and steps that reproduce the problem. Include the export format for export issues. Send screenshots or scans only if you are comfortable sharing their contents with support.

Contact support

BeyondXR is published by Amakat Solutions. For support or privacy questions, call +91 90308 45050.

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. A GitHub account is required.

Requests are public. Do not include private photographs, room scans, precise locations, passwords or other sensitive information.
