How BeyondXR uses device permissions, saves your work and connects to online services.

Publisher: Amakat Solutions · Updated 3 October 2026

Data on your device

BeyondXR saves Home layouts, favorites and tool preferences on your device. Individual tools store the scans, documents, photographs and AR projects you create. Experimental Scanner reconstructs its scenes on your iPhone.

Permissions

Features may request camera access for AR and scanning, microphone access for recordings, location and motion for spatial tools, and photo access for imports or saved captures. Document tools may use Contacts to save business cards and Face ID to unlock protected documents. You can manage permissions in iPhone Settings.

Online features

Map, terrain, nearby-place and live-data features contact external services. Map tile and nearby-place requests can disclose the requested area to the provider, along with connection information such as your IP address. Some tools open external links or share information with nearby devices when you use those features.

Astronomy and Apple services

SkyMap retrieves satellite data from CelesTrak and optional star catalogues from GitHub. Solar System retrieves imagery and astronomy data from NASA services and Where the ISS at. These data downloads expose normal connection information, such as your IP address, to the serving providers. SkyMap sends place names you search for to Apple’s geocoding service. When you configure telescope control, the app sends commands and selected sky coordinates to the telescope server you specify.

Importing a photo that is stored in iCloud may cause Apple Photos to download the selected original. This depends on your photo-library and iCloud settings.

Sharing and exports

You choose where to send exported scans and other files through sharing and export controls. The receiving app, device or service controls its copy. Review the destination before sharing photographs, room scans or location information.

Storage and removal

Use each tool's library controls to remove saved content. Removing a tool from the BeyondXR catalogue does not erase its saved projects. Copies exported to Files, Photos or another service remain there until removed separately. Device backups follow your iPhone and iCloud settings.

Feedback

When you submit feedback, your message, category, optional reply email, app version and a random submission identifier are sent through FormSubmit to Amakat Solutions. No scans, photos or location are attached. FormSubmit processes connection information to deliver and protect the service and states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Copies delivered to our support inbox remain until deleted by Amakat Solutions. Contact amakatsolutions@gmail.com for feedback or deletion requests.

Subscriptions

Apple handles subscription payments and purchase records. BeyondXR uses Apple StoreKit to verify access to paid features. BeyondXR does not receive your payment card details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information to serve and secure the website. These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising scripts or cookies.
